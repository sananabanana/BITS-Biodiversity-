📖 About This Atlas

Welcome to the BITS Hyderabad Biodiversity Atlas — a comprehensive, interactive mapping platform documenting the rich ecological diversity across our campus. This atlas combines advanced GIS technology, field surveys, and biodiversity indices to provide a detailed overview of plant and animal species distribution across different habitat types including forested areas, urban zones, gardens, and water bodies.

Through systematic surveying and data analysis, we have mapped over 6,000 individual observations across the campus, providing insights into species composition, habitat preferences, and ecological diversity patterns. Click on map markers to explore species information, view comparative charts, and understand the biodiversity metrics that characterize our campus ecosystem.

📋 Survey Methodology

  • Spatial Framework: Interactive GIS mapping using Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap tiles for precise georeferencing
  • Forested Areas: 50×50m quadrat sampling across three zones for comprehensive vegetation and fauna surveys
  • Urban Corridors: 100m line transects along roads and built-up areas to assess adaptation and diversity
  • Gardens & Water Bodies: Strategic point sampling at key locations (gardens, lawns, ponds) for habitat-specific documentation
  • Data Analysis: Shannon-Wiener, Simpson's, and Evenness indices calculated to quantify biodiversity patterns

Key Insights
The Shannon-Wiener index (H′ = 1.37) and Simpson's diversity (1−D = 0.54) reveal moderate overall diversity with significant compositional skew. Trees constitute approximately 50% of recorded species, while terrestrial plants account for 25%. The evenness index (J = 0.59) confirms imbalanced abundance distribution across taxonomic groups, suggesting opportunities for biodiversity enhancement.
